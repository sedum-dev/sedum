City council approves riverfront park after two-year debate

By Elena Ruiz · September 24, 2026

The council voted 7–2 on Tuesday to accept the final design for the 14-acre park along the east bank. Residents who attended the hearing were asked to accept a revised timeline that pushes the opening to 2028.

Councilmember Okafor said the city will manage the site jointly with the parks trust. "We had to reject the first plan because it left no room for the boat launch," she said.
